🔤 Word Search Game

Welcome!

Test your word-finding skills in this fun themed word search!

How to Play:

  • A random theme is chosen for each round (Animals, Food, or School)
  • A 6x6 letter grid will be displayed with hidden words inside
  • Words are hidden horizontally or vertically in the grid
  • You will see a list of words to find and a helpful clue hint
  • 🖱️ Click the letters in the grid one by one to spell the word
  • ⌨️ Or type the word in the box and press Guess!
  • Selected letters highlight yellow — found words turn green
  • Each correct word earns you 10 points
  • Find all the words to reveal the mystery location! 🕵️
